# Budget and timeline — Branco Weiss Fellowship (2027 round)

## What the funder requires

- **No budget is submitted with the application** (official application page: "budgets do not have to be submitted"). The proposal must include timelines and tentative milestones; those are in proposal.md, section 6, and repeated below with more detail.
- **Funding envelope:** maximum **CHF 600,000 in total for up to five years**, usable for all legitimate costs of research — salary and/or equipment, travel, consumables, personnel — with the host allowed a management fee of at most 10% (official eligibility/funding page and Guidelines PDF; earlier candidate note of "CHF 100,000 per year" is a per-year rounding of the same cap, not a separate figure). Salary may be covered fully, or partially where the host funds the employment. Fellows are encouraged to acquire third-party funding during the fellowship.
- **Phases:** pioneer phase (first 2.5 years) → site visit and review before the end of year 2 → exploitation phase (remaining 2.5 years), conditional on the review.

The planning budget below is for the applicant and the host only; it is not an application document. All figures are placeholders to be set with the MIE research office.
