It is a five-year portable fellowship of up to CHF 600,000 in total, roughly ten awards a year worldwide, for projects that depart from the mainstream of their discipline and engage with society beyond it. I have written it to be clearly distinct from both my thesis and your two NSERC programmes; I would value your reading on whether that distinction is convincing.

Three questions:
1. Would you host, and can MIE administer a five-year grant under ETH Zurich's three-way agreement (host management fee capped at 10%)? I will contact the research office once you say yes.
2. The programme guidelines say any academic institution worldwide may host. I did my PhD at U of T; do you know of any precedent for or against holding it at the PhD-granting institution? I will ask ETH Zurich directly as well.
3. Could you suggest, without being one of them, two or three senior people in applied ontology or robot learning who are scientifically unrelated to me? The form asks for five independent reviewer suggestions.

A preliminary note of willingness by 1 December would let me finalize the proposal; a signed letter by 8 January would let me submit on the 10th. The draft proposal and motivation letter are attached.

Best,
