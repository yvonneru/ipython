## A. Brief for Prof. Grüninger — the host invitation letter

**What the programme is.** A five-year, portable postdoctoral fellowship of up to CHF 600,000 total (salary and/or research costs), administered by ETH Zurich through a three-way agreement between the fellow, the host institution and ETH Zurich; the host may retain a management fee of at most 10%. Roughly ten fellowships are awarded per year worldwide. The project must depart from the mainstream of the discipline and the fellow must be willing to engage with society beyond the discipline. First 2.5 years are the "pioneer phase"; a site visit and review before the end of year 2 gates the "exploitation phase".

**What the letter must state (from the application page).** Why I am invited; why the research project is supported; that I will be integrated into the research group; that I will have the independence to run the project. One page is normal. [Confirm whether the portal expects a template or specific signatories.]

**The four points that would help most.**
1. That the verified-ontology methodology with working tool support (COLORE, Prover9/Mace4 pipelines, PSL, TUpper) exists in the Semantic Technologies Laboratory and is what Objectives O1–O2 require.
2. That the project is my own and is distinct from both my thesis and your NSERC programmes ("Ontologies for the Physical Turing Test"; "Commonsense Cobotics"): it audits manipulation data at dataset scale and publishes the results as public evidence; yours propose ontological analysis of vision and reasoning benchmarks.
3. That the host will provide independence: my own project budget, freedom to publish and release openly, ability to co-supervise students on the ontology and audit components, and links to the U of T Robotics Institute and the Vector Institute for the learning component.
4. That the department will administer the grant under the three-way contract and accepts the management-fee cap [confirm with the MIE research office / U of T Research Services].

**Ask by:** preliminary willingness note by 1 December 2026; signed letter by 8 January 2027 so I can submit by 10 January.

## C. Brief for reference-letter writers (only if shortlisted, expected May 2027)

**What the award is.** The Branco Weiss Fellowship – Society in Science (ETH Zurich): up to five years, up to CHF 600,000 total, held at [University of Toronto, MIE] under Prof. Michael Grüninger as host, start between 1 October and 31 December 2027 by precedent. Interviews in Zurich in [June 2027 by precedent].

**What the Fellowship weighs.** (1) A record of outstanding scientific achievement relative to career stage. (2) A project that departs from the mainstream of the discipline and is original and independent — not a continuation of the PhD. (3) Willingness to engage in a dialogue on social, cultural, political or economic issues beyond the discipline. Letters that speak to all three, with one or two concrete examples, are the most useful.

**Points that would help.**
1. The theoretical work on material constitution and mereological pluralism (two Synthese submissions), and why a machine-checkable theory of multiple parthood relations is new.
2. The ISO/IEC 21838-4 contribution: what it shows about rigour and about working in a standards body — the route by which the audit becomes public evidence.
3. The move from theory to deployed systems (patents, production data integration, the Vision Pro application) and why that predicts delivery of a dataset-scale audit.
4. Independence and departure: the project is the applicant's own; the thesis was on knowledge-system architecture, not on parts, datasets or policies.

**Deadline.** Set by the Fellowship when it requests the letters [expected within weeks of the May 2027 shortlist notification]. I will send the final proposal, motivation letter and CV with the request.
