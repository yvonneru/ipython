# Branco Weiss Fellowship – Society in Science (2027 round) — application brief

**Applicant:** Dr. Yi Ru (individual application; no nominator). **Funder:** The Branco Weiss Fellowship – Society in Science, administered by ETH Zurich. **Official site:** https://brancoweissfellowship.org/ · **Registry:** funding/registry/opportunities.json, slug `branco-weiss-fellowship-society-in-science-2027` (tier B, fit 68). **Package drafted:** 2026-09-25.

## Programme in one paragraph

A portable postdoctoral fellowship for researchers after the PhD and before a first faculty post: up to **CHF 600,000 in total for up to five years**, held at any academic institution worldwide of the fellow's choosing, for a project that **departs from the mainstream of its discipline** and whose holder is willing to **engage in dialogue on social, cultural, political or economic issues beyond the discipline**. About ten fellowships are awarded per year worldwide [figure from the registry record; confirm on the site]. The first 2.5 years are a "pioneer phase"; a site visit and review before the end of year 2 gates the "exploitation phase" [from the Guidelines PDF as recorded in the registry; re-read the 2027 Guidelines when the portal opens].
