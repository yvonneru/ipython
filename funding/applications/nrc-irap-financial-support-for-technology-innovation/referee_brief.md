# Brief for letter-of-support writers (IRAP has no referees)

IRAP does not use academic referees. The ITA's assessment relies on the company's own documents, a site visit or technical review, and — where the ITA asks for them — **letters of support from customers, co-development partners, assessment bodies or research collaborators**, and customer reference calls. This brief is for the people we will ask for such letters. Send it with the request (emails 3–5 in emails.md), together with the one-page company profile and section 2 (objectives) and the milestone table of the proposal.

## What the program is

NRC IRAP (National Research Council Canada, Industrial Research Assistance Program) provides non-dilutive contributions to Canadian SMEs for R&D projects with technical uncertainty, typically CAD 50k–500k for early projects, covering up to 80% of R&D salaries and 50% of contractor costs, negotiated with an Industrial Technology Advisor. AXIOMALITY is applying for a 12-month project, *Verification Engine v1 and Data Passport*, to take its robot-data verification Engine from internal test to a product running on customer data, with a portable evidence Passport evaluated by an assessment body. We expect to submit the proposal to the ITA by 30 November 2026 and to start the project on signature of the contribution agreement, targeted for April 2027 or earlier [confirm with ITA].

## What the ITA weighs (conventional — verify)

Technical merit and technological uncertainty; technical feasibility and the work plan; management and team capability; commercial potential and the business plan; financial capacity; and benefits to Canada. Weights are not published; the ITA's recommendation decides. A letter of support is most useful for **commercial potential** (a named buyer with a real, recurring data decision) and for **technical feasibility** (a partner who will supply data and run an independent acceptance test).

## What the letter should say (one page, on letterhead)

1. Who you are and what data decision you own (model developer, robot OEM, data supplier, industrial integrator, assessment body, research laboratory).
2. The problem in your own words — for example: heterogeneous demonstrations, field logs and generated data arrive with different assumptions; review time is consumed by coordinate errors, duplicates, missing metadata and inconsistent task labels; a failed deployment needs the source record, a localized cause and a reproducible regression test; releases need versioned checks connected to evidence.
3. What you will do with AXIOMALITY in the project: the dataset(s) you will supply [format: LeRobot / RLDS / ROS 2 MCAP / OpenUSD; approximate episode count]; the task family; the acceptance test you will run independently and its baseline (your current scripts or review workflow); the person who will be the project contact.
4. Commercial intent, stated as precisely as you can: interest in a pilot (USD 25–50k, 8–12 weeks, one task family, fixed data and acceptance) and, on acceptance, an annual deployment (USD 120–240k). A signed or conditional commitment weighs more than interest; if you can only express interest, say what acceptance result would convert it.
5. For an assessment body or standards organization: willingness to evaluate Passport v1 in writing against [your scheme / the relevant ISO 10218 / 13482, NIST AI RMF, or ANSI/A3 R15.06/R15.08 reference], and what the evaluation would cover.
6. For a research collaborator (University of Toronto Semantic Technologies Laboratory): the scope of the contracted verification work, its deliverables, and the IP terms under the research agreement.

## The four points that would help most

1. **A dated, recurring data decision.** "We release a model / accept a data batch every [n] weeks and spend about [hours] per batch on review." This turns commercial potential from a market estimate into a named account.
2. **Independent acceptance.** State that you will hold the test set and the baseline, and that acceptance thresholds are agreed before delivery. ITAs discount self-attested results.
3. **Canadian benefit.** If you operate in Canada, say so — jobs, sites, exports affected. If you are a US buyer, say that a Canadian supplier of verified data or evidence would be procured.
4. **Why this team.** One sentence on the machine-proven kernel, the ISO/IEC 21838-4 lineage, or the founder's execution record (MICAS, Vision Pro app), if you can speak to it from your own contact with us.
