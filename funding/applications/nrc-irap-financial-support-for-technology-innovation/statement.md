# Cover letter to the Industrial Technology Advisor and founder's statement

IRAP has no personal-statement component. Two texts are needed instead: (A) a cover letter that accompanies the proposal when it goes to the assigned ITA, and (B) a founder's statement of management capability and Canadian commitment that answers the questions an ITA asks at intake and site visit. Items in [brackets] must be supplied by the applicant.

AXIOMALITY builds the verification and evaluation layer for embodied-AI data. Robot-learning datasets are scarce, expensive to collect, bound to the robot that collected them, and combined in training pipelines without any check that their part, contact and task annotations are consistent with one another or with physics. From 2 August 2028 the EU AI Act's Annex I obligations apply to AI embedded in robots and require traceable, auditable training data; US and Canadian buyers already gate their own data through acceptance and regression workflows. Our Engine checks each record against an axiom set that has been machine-proven consistent and aligned with ISO/IEC 21838-4, and our Passport packages the result so that a customer, an assessor or a regulator can rerun the check.

The proposed 12-month project takes the Engine from internal test to a product running on customer data, with four milestones: a frozen, verified kernel v1 (month 3); 10,000 verified episodes across partner and open datasets (month 6); Passport v1 evaluated in writing by one assessment body or standards organization (month 9); and pre-registered validation experiments with [two] design partners that decide, on evidence, which expansion modules enter the product (month 12). The technological uncertainties are stated plainly in section 3 of the proposal: the compiled checking subset must be shown to preserve the meaning of the first-order constraints; physics residuals need an observation contract; conformal coverage is not per-trajectory safety; and whether semantic checks beat simple rules at equal cost is a question only the experiments can answer.
