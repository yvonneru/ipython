## Statement of career goals

My goal is a faculty position in knowledge representation for engineering systems: a laboratory in which formally verified ontologies of the physical world are built, proved correct, and used to specify, audit and constrain the learned systems — robots, digital twins, product models — that engineering now depends on. The Research Excellence Postdoctoral Fellowship is the step that goal requires, because it funds an independent program on a two-year horizon with research funds that are mine to direct.

**Where I am.** I completed my PhD in Information Engineering at the University of Toronto in March 2025 [confirm date all requirements were met], after a BASc in Industrial Engineering at the same university. My doctoral work developed an architecture for AI knowledge systems in which a verified ontology governs the data model, the learned models and the simulation layer, supervised by [name]. During the doctorate I became a core contributor to ISO/IEC 21838-4:2023, the international standard for the TUpper top-level ontology, and, with Prof. Michael Grüninger, began the line of work on material constitution and mereological pluralism that has now produced two first-author journal submissions to Synthese. Since [month year] I have been a postdoctoral researcher at Harvard Medical School, in [laboratory/department], working on [one sentence]. That appointment has given me a second research environment, a different data regime and an applied community that judges formal methods by whether they work; it has also confirmed for me that the open problem I most want to work on is the one described in my proposal, and that the right place to do it is Toronto.

**What I have done outside the university, and why it matters for the plan.** Before and during my doctorate I founded or co-founded technology companies in which I was responsible for the algorithmic systems and their data architecture: production recommendation, CRM/ERP and data-integration systems whose data models were ontology-governed, and structured 3D datasets of objects and indoor environments for embodied AI, from which I hold patents as first inventor [reconcile counts]. The engineering discipline this required — versioned axioms, regression tests on inferences, reproducible pipelines — is the discipline the audit and learning components of my proposal depend on, and it is why I can commit to public releases of data and tools rather than a single result.

**What the fellowship will produce.** By the end of the two years I intend to have (i) a verified parts ontology contributed to COLORE and to ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 32; (ii) the first quantitative audit of parthood consistency in the datasets that physical-AI systems are trained on, with corrected annotations, cross-dataset mappings and a merged corpus in public release; (iii) results on whether ontology constraints improve manipulation-policy generalization, published at machine-learning and robotics venues; and (iv) a record of co-supervising MIE graduate students and mentoring undergraduate researchers. These are the outputs a hiring committee in engineering or computer science reads: a research agenda that is demonstrably my own, artifacts the field uses, and evidence that I can train students.

**Why this environment.** The verified-ontology methodology with tool support — modular first-order theories, machine-checked consistency, representation theorems, the COLORE repository — was developed in Prof. Grüninger's Semantic Technologies Laboratory and is practised, with working tool support, nowhere else; objectives 1 and 2 of my proposal cannot be executed without it. I completed my doctorate at the same university, under [doctoral supervisor — name; must not be Prof. Grüninger or the co-mentor, since the program bars former PhD students of the proposed supervisor or co-supervisor — verify on the FASE page], so I address the choice directly: I return to Toronto not for continuity but for a specific capability, with a program that is my own — my thesis was on knowledge-system architecture and top-level ontology; the proposal is on physical object parts in robot data, and none of its datasets, methods or experiments appears in my thesis. The Faculty supplies the second half of the program: the U of T Robotics Institute and the Vector Institute provide the robotics community, collaborators and compute that objective 3 needs, and the co-mentorship of Prof. [name] gives the learning component a home.

**Development during the fellowship.** With Prof. Grüninger I will follow a mentoring plan built around weekly supervision, co-supervision of graduate students on the ontology and audit components, the Robotics Institute seminar series, a Vector Institute affiliation for compute and for the machine-learning component, representation of the laboratory at ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 32 meetings, and [the faculty-preparation programming that MIE, FASE or SGS offers postdoctoral fellows — name the actual series after confirming with Prof. Grüninger or the MIE graduate office]. I will mentor [number] undergraduate research students, [from an equity-deserving group where possible — applicant to specify the program and commitment], and I will release every axiom, mapping, dataset correction and line of evaluation code openly so that groups without access to proprietary robot data can build on the work. Ontology-driven data integration is what I do; the fellowship lets me do it on the datasets that will define physical AI, in the one laboratory equipped to prove the results.
