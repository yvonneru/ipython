## Program in one paragraph
The Research Excellence Postdoctoral Fellows Program is a University of Toronto internal award (Office of the Vice-President Research & Innovation / Provost) administered by the divisions; the Faculty of Applied Science & Engineering (FASE) runs its own round through FASE Graduate Studies. It pays CAD 80,000 per year salary plus employer costs for two years, and gives the fellow CAD 10,000 per year of independent research funds. Candidates may be domestic or international, must be within five years of PhD completion at the start date, must not hold a faculty position, and are nominated through a U of T division with a faculty supervisor. The 2026 round's start window was 1 April 2026 – 31 March 2027 (start within 12 months of offer). The program was announced as a two-year, 100-fellow initiative (2025-26 and 2026-27 rounds), so **a 2027 round is not guaranteed and must be confirmed with FASE Graduate Studies in November–December 2026**. Official page: https://gradstudies.engineering.utoronto.ca/prospective-students/money-matters/research-excellence-postdoctoral-fellows-program/
