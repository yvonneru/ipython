## Program in one paragraph
The Research Excellence Postdoctoral Fellows Program is a University of Toronto internal award (Office of the Vice-President Research & Innovation / Provost) administered by the divisions; the Faculty of Applied Science & Engineering (FASE) runs its own round through FASE Graduate Studies. It pays CAD 80,000 per year salary plus employer costs for two years, and gives the fellow CAD 10,000 per year of independent research funds. Per the FASE page (search snippet, 2026-09-25), 60% of the value is institutional support and 40% is funded from the supervisor's grant. Candidates may be domestic or international; must have completed the PhD before the start date (the UTM page adds: within five years of the PhD); must not have held more than one previous postdoctoral appointment; must not be a current or former PhD student of the proposed supervisor or co-supervisor; must not hold a faculty position; and are nominated through a U of T division with a faculty supervisor. The 2026 round's FASE deadline was 4 February 2026 with a start window of 1 April 2026 – 31 March 2027. The program was announced as a two-year, 100-fellow initiative (2025-26 and 2026-27 rounds); UTM (departmental deadline 9 November 2026) and UTSC (applications late September – early November 2026, per the completeness review) are running their second rounds this autumn, so **the FASE second round may be open now and must be confirmed with FASE Graduate Studies this week (email 2)**. FASE contact named on the page per a search snippet: David Duong, Engineering Graduate Affairs Officer, d.duong@utoronto.ca [verify]. Official page: https://gradstudies.engineering.utoronto.ca/prospective-students/money-matters/research-excellence-postdoctoral-fellows-program/
