## What the program is

Creative Destruction Lab (Rotman School of Management, University of Toronto) is a nine-month, objectives-based program for seed-stage science- and technology-based ventures. No fee, no equity. Founders attend five day-long sessions between October and June at which mentors — experienced founders, investors and scientists — set three objectives per company and review them at each session; mentors and CDL associates may invest at their discretion. The Artificial Intelligence stream selects founders building AI systems that generate hypotheses, simulate experiments, optimize design processes, or autonomously interact with and learn from complex environments, including robotic control. CDL's stated sweet spot is a venture planning a US$500K–5M seed round within 12 months of applying.

## What reviewers weigh (no published weights)

Technology that is novel, defensible and scalable with "exciting potential"; a team well positioned to drive the technology's success; an "unfair advantage" (the FAQ's examples: a patent, a world leader on the founding team, unique access to data); momentum or upward trajectory; readiness to raise a seed round [sweet spot unverified]. CDL's own guidance to applicants: explain passion for the problem, be honest about challenges, do not oversell business experience, do not undersell excellence in any domain.
