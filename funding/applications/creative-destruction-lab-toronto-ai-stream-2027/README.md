## Program

Creative Destruction Lab (CDL) is a nine-month, objectives-based program for seed-stage, massively scalable science- and technology-based ventures, run from the Rotman School of Management at the University of Toronto and at partner sites. CDL takes no equity and charges no fee; founders attend five day-long sessions (October to June) at which mentors (experienced founders, investors, scientists) set and review three objectives per company, and mentors and CDL associates may invest at their own discretion. The AI stream selects founders "developing AI systems that generate hypotheses, simulate experiments, optimize design processes, or autonomously interact with and learn from complex environments – such as in lab automation, material development, or robotic control." CDL's stated sweet spot is a venture that plans to raise a seed round of approximately US$500K–5M within 12 months of applying; most admitted ventures have two or more co-founders and are beyond the concept stage with a prototype in development.

This package positions AXIOMALITY — the verification and evidence layer for robot-learning data (offline Engine, data Passport, four-layer ontology kernel aligned with ISO/IEC 21838-4) — as an AI-for-robotics venture whose product is the machine-checkable evidence that every data generator and every deployment needs. It is written as a company application from the September 2026 US/Canada deck and the company brief; the founder's academic applications (CPRA, Vector, DSI, CIRTA) are separate and are mentioned only where a conflict-of-commitment disclosure is needed.
