## Program

Amazon Research Awards (ARA) fund university research through **unrestricted cash gifts to the PI's institution plus AWS Promotional Credits**, awarded per topical call for proposals (Amazon Science, program rules and FAQ, checked 2026-09-25). The cash component "will be structured as a one-time unrestricted gift to the Principal Investigator's academic institution … and may not be used for indirect expenses which are not allocable, reasonable, adequately documented, and consistent with established policies" (program rules). A proposed cash budget "is generally anticipated to support one to two graduate students or a post-doctoral researcher for one year, plus some conference travel and equipment" (FAQ). Duration 12 months. Ceiling: the Fall 2025 call advertised up to USD 100,000 [confirm the Fall 2026 ceiling and the cash/credit split in the call text]. Decisions "approximately three months after the submission deadline" (FAQ); Fall 2025 decision letters went out March 2026.
