## Program in one paragraph
The Klarman Fellowships are Cornell's college-wide postdoctoral fellowships in the College of Arts & Sciences, open to any discipline in the college. Registry facts (deadline confidence high): USD 80,000/yr stipend plus benefits and USD 12,000/yr research funds; up to three years; start between 1 July and 1 September 2027; annual cycle with the portal open 15 August – 15 October. The fellow must identify a faculty host with a primary A&S appointment; Cornell CS faculty commonly hold A&S appointments. Official page: https://as.cornell.edu/research/klarman-fellowships
