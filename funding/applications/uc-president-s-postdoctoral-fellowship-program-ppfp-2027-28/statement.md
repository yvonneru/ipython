My path through higher education was not a straight line, and I think that is its main value. From 2018 to 2021 I co-founded YourTable Inc., a recommendation-system company incubated by the University of Toronto, the Schulich School of Business and Imperial College London, which received the Lo Family Social Venture Fund and represented Canadian startups at Collision 2019. From 2021 to 2022 I was on the founding team of MICAS, where I led a team of more than one hundred people and built the CRM, ERP and warehouse systems of a business with more than USD 50 million in annual revenue. I returned from each of these to formal logic with a sharper question: a theory that cannot be checked by a machine and cannot survive contact with real data is not yet finished. That conviction is why parts of my doctoral work went into an international standard, and why the research I propose treats the datasets that robot-learning models are trained on as objects that can be audited against a specification.
