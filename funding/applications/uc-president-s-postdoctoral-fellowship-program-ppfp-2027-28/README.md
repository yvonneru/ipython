## Program in one paragraph
PPFP, run by the University of California Office of the President, funds postdoctoral scholars at UC campuses whose research, teaching and service prepare them for faculty careers; UC departments that hire former fellows into ladder-rank positions have had access to a hiring incentive [confirm current form]. Any nationality; PhD required by the start date; the applicant must secure a **tenured UC faculty mentor other than the dissertation advisor**. Award (2026 figures): salary from about USD 69,209 depending on field and experience, USD 5,000 research/professional development, benefits; one year [renewable for a second — confirm]. Official pages: https://ppfp.ucop.edu/ppfp/ and https://ppfp.ucop.edu/info/.
