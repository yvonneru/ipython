## Format and criteria (verify on the official page)

Confirmed by web search during the 2026-09-25 review (NSERC review committee guide for the CGRS-Doctoral and CPRA programs; NSERC form 201 instructions; NSERC instructions for completing a reference letter — form 201; NSERC CPRA program page):
- **Selection criteria and weights: 50% research potential and experience of the applicant; 50% quality of the proposed research program.** EDI integrated in the research design is assessed as part of the second criterion. Contributions are assessed on quality and impact, not number. Sub-criteria and indicators are in the guide's "Selection criteria and indicators" section and Appendix B (CPRA) — read Appendix B before the 2 Oct draft.
- Deadline 17 Oct 2026, 8 pm ET, form 201 in the NSERC online system; no changes after submission; applicants are told to confirm the deadline with their faculty of graduate studies.
- Reference letters: two text boxes, about half a page and three-quarters of a page.
- Attachments follow NSERC's on-line presentation and attachment standards (linked from the form 201 instructions); the exact page limits per attachment were **not** confirmed by search — the limits below come from the applicant's checklist and must be checked against that page.

Recorded format (from the registry entry and the applicant's checklist):
- Attachments as PDF, Letter size, **12 pt Times New Roman, 0.75 in margins**, name top right, page numbers.
- **Outline of proposed research: 2 pages.** **Bibliography: 1 page.** **Contributions and statements: 4 pages.** Justification for eligibility of proposed research: 1 page, optional (read by staff, not reviewers). Special circumstances: optional.
- Official doctoral transcript showing the requirements-met date, or transcript plus SGS letter. Proof of citizenship / immigration status.
- Form 201 sections: application profile, personal profile, addresses, academic background (requirements-met date), experience, awards (up to 10), proposed location(s) of tenure (up to 3), justification for location of tenure, theses completed, keywords and research subject code, EDI considerations, self-identification questionnaire (mandatory), priority research areas.
- Two referee reports, submitted in the system in two text boxes (research ability and potential; relevant skills and experience). Character limits shown on screen [verify].
- Review criteria (confirmed, see above): **research potential and experience 50%; quality of the proposed research program 50%**, EDI-in-design inside the second. The funder does not prescribe headings for the outline, so the Grüninger structure is used.
