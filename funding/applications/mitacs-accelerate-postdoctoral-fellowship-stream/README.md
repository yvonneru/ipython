## Program

Mitacs Accelerate funds research internships in which a postdoctoral fellow at a Canadian university works on a project jointly defined with a partner organization. Mitacs Elevate, the former postdoc-specific program, ceased on 31 March 2025; postdocs now apply through Accelerate. Funding is organized in **internship units of 4–6 months at CAD 20,000 per unit**; up to 3 units per year and up to 9 units (3 years) per intern; the partner contributes roughly half of each unit (about CAD 10,000 per unit) in cash and Mitacs supplies the rest. Intake is continuous; peer review takes 6–8 weeks; Mitacs recommends applying about 16 weeks before the intended start. Official page recorded in the registry: https://www.mitacs.ca/our-programs/accelerate/

This package proposes a **six-unit, 24-month project** (three units per year) under the title *"Verified part-level data for physical AI: an ontology-based audit and alignment service for robot-learning datasets."* It is built from Objectives O1–O3 of the applicant's research program, with the partner-facing deliverables concentrated on O2 (the audit and alignment pipeline) because that is what a partner can use in each unit.

The registry treats Accelerate Entrepreneur as a separate opportunity (fit 78); if route B is chosen, this package's proposal.md is reused with the "Route B" paragraphs and the incubator letter added.
