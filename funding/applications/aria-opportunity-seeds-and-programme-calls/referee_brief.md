## What the program is
ARIA (UK Advanced Research and Invention Agency) funds high-risk, high-reward research through Programme Directors. The Rolling Opportunity Seeds call (solicitation dated 29 May 2026) offers GBP 10,000–500,000 per project, inclusive of VAT and all costs, across selected opportunity spaces [verify current list; this proposal targets Mathematics for Safe AI or Smarter Robot Bodies]. Proposals are reviewed monthly by the Programme Director and at least one other reviewer. International applicants are eligible, but ARIA funds those based in the UK or conducting more than 50% of the project from the UK (otherwise only exceptionally, on a UK-benefit case), which is why the project is led from [UK host].

## What the form asks
An online form with short answers [verify field list and limits]: the idea, why it matters, why now, what will be built, the team, milestones, budget in GBP, and where the work is done. No concept paper for seeds; a 3-page concept paper applies only to programme calls.

## What reviewers weigh
ARIA's published seed process: a format-compliance check (non-compliant proposals are rejected unread), then substantive review by the Programme Director and at least one other reviewer, with final selection approved by ARIA's CTO. The stated criteria (no weights): a bold idea that could change the conversation about what is possible or valuable; how the idea aligns with or challenges the Summary, Beliefs or Observations of the opportunity-space document; feasibility of the plan and team; fit with the portfolio and between team types; and, for applicants not operating in the UK, the extent to which the work benefits the UK. The most useful support letter is one paragraph long, specific, and says what you will actually contribute or provide.
