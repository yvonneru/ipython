## 1. The program, and the three routes it actually offers

ARIA (the UK Advanced Research and Invention Agency) funds "bold ideas that could change the conversation about what is possible or valuable" through Programme Directors who run *opportunity spaces* and *programmes*. Three things are bundled under this registry entry; only the first two are routes an applicant can take.

| Route | What it is (web 2026-09-25) | Relevance to this applicant |
|---|---|---|
| **Rolling Opportunity Seeds** | A rolling call for proposals ("Rolling Opportunity Seeds Call for proposals, 29 May 2026, v1.0") across *selected* opportunity spaces: **GBP 10,000–500,000** per project inclusive of VAT and all costs; open continuously, **reviewed monthly**; no minimum project length, maximum three years; a short online form (ARIA: two to three hours to complete); applicants may be invited to meet the Programme Director before selection. Seed calls do **not** use concept papers. Search results name Smarter Robot Bodies, Programmable Plants and Mathematics for Safe AI / Safeguarded AI among the spaces with live seed funding; the list "may change over time", so the current PDF is authoritative. | **Primary route.** The proposal is a seed under *Mathematics for Safe AI* [primary — its thesis, machine-checkable evidence for AI safety, is this seed's thesis; verify seeds are open there] or *Smarter Robot Bodies* [secondary — its thesis is robot bodies built from AI, control, materials and manufacturing, so a data-assurance seed fits only if argued against that document's Beliefs/Observations]. The only Safeguarded AI call confirmed open in Sept 2026 is the TA2 *Cybersecurity* Blue-Team call (GBP 2.5–3.5M per team; rolling, next cut-off 31 Oct 2026 14:00 GMT per the registry) — off-topic for this program. | **Secondary / watch.** No open programme call matches robot data quality as of this draft. | **Activation Partners** | Organisations ARIA funds to support its *already funded* teams ("creators") with translation, engineering and commercial help [search snippet, unverified: one partner reported supporting about 30 ARIA-funded teams including robotics teams]. | **Not an application route.** Becomes relevant only after an ARIA award, when AXIOMALITY could be matched with an Activation Partner for productisation. ## 4. Review criteria and weights

ARIA publishes no numeric weights; it describes a "criteria-led" selection in which proposals are expected to "spike" on some criteria rather than score evenly. From ARIA's *opportunity seed review and selection process* document, the Rolling Seeds call and the applicant guidance (search snippets, 2026-09-25):

**Process.** (0) A format-compliance check by ARIA staff — non-compliant proposals are rejected before review; (1) substantive review by the Programme Director and at least one other reviewer, with internal or external experts added for conflicts or specific expertise; applicants may be invited to a meeting or asked clarification questions; (2) final seed selection approved by ARIA's CTO; monthly cycle.
