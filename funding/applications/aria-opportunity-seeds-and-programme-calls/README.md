## 1. The program, and the three routes it actually offers

ARIA (the UK Advanced Research and Invention Agency) funds "bold ideas that could change the conversation about what is possible or valuable" through Programme Directors who run *opportunity spaces* and *programmes*. Three things are bundled under this registry entry; only the first two are routes an applicant can take.

## 4. Review criteria and weights

ARIA publishes no numeric weights. From the 2024 Robot Dexterity proposal guidelines and the FAQ (web 2026-09-25) the criteria a Programme Director applies are: (1) **feasibility** of the plan; (2) **relevance** to the opportunity space / programme thesis; (3) **fit with the overall portfolio** of funded projects; (4) **fit between team types** (ARIA deliberately mixes academic, start-up and industrial teams); (5) **results over academic papers**; and, for seeds, whether the idea "could change the conversation about what is possible or valuable".
