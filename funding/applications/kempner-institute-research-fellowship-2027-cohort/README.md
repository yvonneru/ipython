## Program in one paragraph

An independent postdoctoral research fellowship at Harvard's Kempner Institute, for researchers working on the foundations of intelligence in natural and artificial systems or on applications of AI. Salary (about USD 100k reported; [confirm]) plus research support, office space, compute and engineering support, for up to three years, starting September 2027. Fellows propose 2–3 mentors from Kempner-affiliated faculty or Harvard researchers and run their own program. Official page: https://kempnerinstitute.harvard.edu/kempner-institute-research-fellowship
