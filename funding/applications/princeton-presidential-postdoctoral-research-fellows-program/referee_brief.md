## What the selection committee weighs
The program is university-wide and highly competitive across all disciplines, so the committee compares candidates from fields it cannot judge technically. The published selection language [verify exact wording on the official page; the program's criteria were revised in 2025] emphasizes research excellence — the quality, originality and significance of the proposed research and the candidate's promise as a scholar — and the candidate's contributions to the academic community through mentoring, teaching, service and outreach, together with the fit and commitment of the faculty host. The most useful letters therefore (a) explain the work in terms a scholar from another field will understand, (b) give one or two concrete examples — a result you saw me obtain, a problem I solved without direction, a piece of work you would not have expected at my stage — and (c) state plainly where I stand relative to other early-career researchers you have known. If you can speak to mentoring, teaching or service you have seen me do, one concrete example is worth more than a general endorsement.
