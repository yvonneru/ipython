## Program in one paragraph
The Presidential Postdoctoral Research Fellows Program is run by Princeton University's Office of the Dean of the Faculty. Registry facts (deadline confidence **low**): a Princeton postdoctoral salary (approx. USD 70,000–80,000) plus benefits and research funds; appointments of up to three years; September 2027 start; open to recent PhDs (typically within about three years) in any discipline, of any nationality; requires a Princeton faculty sponsor/host; selection emphasizes research excellence and contributions to the academic community. Official page: https://dof.princeton.edu/presidential-postdoctoral-research-fellows-program
