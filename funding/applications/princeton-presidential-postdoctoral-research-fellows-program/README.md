## Program in one paragraph
The Presidential Postdoctoral Research Fellows Program is run by Princeton University's Office of the Dean of the Faculty as a university-wide "talent pathway" program. From Princeton department and DOF pages found on 2026-09-25 (three web searches; some figures may be from a prior cycle — verify against the call distributed in early September 2026): it is **nomination-only** — a Princeton assistant, associate or full professor nominates a candidate and serves as sponsor/PI, and **each sponsor may nominate one candidate**; about **twelve fellows** are appointed per year (fifteen were named in October 2025); the appointment is **one year, renewable for a second**, with full salary for up to two years (a Princeton lab page cites **USD 75,000** plus support for computing, professional development and research); candidates must hold a **PhD within 4 years of the start date**, which falls between **1 July and 1 September** of the cohort year; **all nominations and materials are due 13 November**. The program "recognizes and supports outstanding scholars primed to make important contributions in their fields" who "contribute to the University's diversity, broadly defined" [criteria revised in 2025 — read the current wording]. Official page: https://dof.princeton.edu/faculty-development-and-inclusion/talent-pathway-programs/presidential-postdoctoral-research-fellows
