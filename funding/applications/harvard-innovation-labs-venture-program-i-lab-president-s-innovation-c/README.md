## Program

Two linked opportunities at the Harvard Innovation Labs, both equity-free:

1. **Venture Incubation Program ("Venture Program", VIP).** A 12-week cohort program for Harvard-founded ventures: staff advisor, expert office hours, workspace at the i-lab, partner credits and the i-lab community. No cash. Admission by application, by semester (spring 2027 = roughly January–April 2027 [dates unconfirmed]). Official page: https://innovationlabs.harvard.edu/application/
2. **President's Innovation Challenge (PIC) 2027.** Harvard's flagship venture competition, run by the i-lab with the Office of the President. 2026 cycle: more than USD 500,000 awarded; five grand prizes of USD 75,000 and five prizes of USD 25,000 across the tracks; Ingenuity Awards (up to USD 2,500) for early-stage student ideas. Student-led tracks: **Open**, **Health Care and Life Sciences**, **Social Impact**; two further tracks are reserved for alumni and Life Lab / Launch Lab X affiliate ventures (not available to AXIOMALITY: the founder is a U of T, not a Harvard, graduate). Official pages: https://innovationlabs.harvard.edu/presidents-innovation-challenge and https://innovationlabs.harvard.edu/presidents-innovation-challenge/eligibility
