**There are no reference letters in either program** (registry; no letter requirement was found on the official pages — [confirm on the live form]). ## What the programs are

- **Venture Program (VIP):** a 12-week i-lab cohort program (spring 2027, about January–April [dates unconfirmed]) giving a staff advisor, expert office hours, workspace and partner credits; no cash. Eligibility: at least one founder who is a current, full-time, matriculated Harvard student or postdoctoral fellow acts as Team Lead; HUID status is checked; the Team Lead must be present at the i-lab through the program.
- **President's Innovation Challenge (PIC) 2027:** Harvard's flagship venture competition; in 2026, five USD 75,000 grand prizes and five USD 25,000 prizes across tracks (Open; Health Care and Life Sciences; Social Impact; plus alumni/affiliate tracks). Applications expected to open in October–November 2026 and close in **early December 2026** [unconfirmed; the 2026 deadline was 8 December 2025]; semifinalists mid-January; finalists in spring; awards ceremony in late April / May 2027.

## What the form asks

Written answers about the venture (problem, solution, market, traction, business model, team, impact), a pitch deck, and a short video [field list, character limits and video length to be recorded from the live form]. No live pitch until the finals.

## What the judges score

From the i-lab's own PIC guidance: the semifinalist cut "focuses significantly on venture stage" — customers, revenue, pilots, partnerships, engaged users, or funding from professional investors; external judges then "rate your application on the criteria ... on a numerical scale", evaluating "business model and traction" and "impact and/or potential", quantified where possible, written for a non-specialist audience. Numerical weights are not published.
