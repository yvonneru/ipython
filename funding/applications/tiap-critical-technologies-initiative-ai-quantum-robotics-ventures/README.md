## Program

TIAP is the venture-building and commercialization organization affiliated with the University of Toronto and its partner teaching hospitals. Its Critical Technologies Initiative (CTI) is a FedDev Ontario-supported stream for early-stage ventures using critical technologies. From the official page as surfaced by web search (verify on the page itself): the CTI "supports emerging life sciences technologies and early-stage ventures utilizing 5G and advanced networks, blockchain, cybersecurity, ethical artificial intelligence (AI), quantum computing, and/or robotics with **up to $200K comprised of a grant of up to $100K plus a dilutive match** from TIAP (for TIAP member institutions) or a match from another third-party institution (for non-TIAP members)", to be applied to technology development and/or executive advisory support. Applications are accepted on an ongoing basis; applicants submit a project proposal with budget and milestones and an investment committee decides.

This package proposes a **12-month project**, *"Passport v1 for regulated robotics: a verified evidence layer for robot and medical-device AI data,"* built from the AXIOMALITY company brief (the September 2026 US/Canada deck) and the founder's research program. It is the company analogue of Objectives O1–O3 of that program (verified ontology kernel → dataset-scale audit → measured effect on learning), scoped to what CAD 100K of non-dilutive funding plus a matched CAD 100K can buy: hardening the Engine after its Q4 2026 internal test, verifying 10,000 episodes, and taking Passport v1 to an assessment body — with a regulated-health design-partner track that fits TIAP's mandate. It is written as a company application; the founder's academic applications (CPRA, Vector, DSI, CIRTA, Mitacs) are separate and appear only where a conflict-of-interest disclosure is needed.

**Sector-scope caution.** Every TIAP self-description found by search says TIAP focuses on health-science commercialization (medical devices, therapeutics, digital/AI health). The CTI text lists AI, quantum and robotics ventures without restricting them to health, and the registry scouted the stream as open to AI/quantum/robotics ventures generally. Whether a robotics-data venture whose first buyers are robot OEMs and model developers qualifies, or whether it must present a health-sciences application, is **unconfirmed** and is the first question for the pre-screen call (emails.md, email 1). The proposal is therefore written so that the regulated-health track (hospital-network and medical-device design partners, which the deck lists as existing relationships) leads if TIAP requires it, while the underlying technology and business are unchanged.
