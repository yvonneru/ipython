## Founder statement

I am a knowledge-representation researcher who has carried formal methods into practice through an international standard, patents and production systems, and AXIOMALITY is the company that puts the three together. I build formally verified first-order ontologies of physical objects and their parts and use them as specifications, audits and inductive biases for robot data and manipulation policies. The standard is ISO/IEC 21838-4:2023 (TUpper), to which I was a core contributor within ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 [role wording to confirm]. The theory is the account of material constitution as a parthood-preserving mapping between mereologies, developed with Prof. Grüninger and submitted to Synthese in 2026: given two part decompositions of the same object, it says exactly what must hold for them to be compatible — which is the question every robot dataset poses when kinematic, functional and visual part labels co-exist without any statement of how they relate. The practice is Uing Technologies, where my team built room-level reconstruction and object-level annotation into a stack that produced 100,000 measured 3D asset packages, nine invention applications and one of the first AR pet-interaction applications on the Apple Vision Pro App Store, and before that MICAS, where as a founding team member I led a 100-plus-person team, built the CRM/ERP/WMS and ML-driven marketing systems, and saw the company pass USD 50 million in annual revenue in its first year on USD 5 million of angel investment including Accel Partners.

I completed my PhD in Information Engineering at the University of Toronto in March 2025 (Fast Track) and am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Harvard Medical School [confirm — not on the CV; laboratory; one sentence on the work], which has placed me inside the data regime of regulated health and confirmed for me that the evidence requirement there is the strictest version of the one robotics faces. AXIOMALITY exists because raw robot hours are approaching zero price while the value moves to semantics, verification and certification, and because from 2 August 2028 the EU AI Act's Annex I obligations apply to AI embedded in robots and other regulated products. None of today's evidence forms can be checked record by record by a machine; ours can.

What I ask of TIAP is what the CTI is for: technology de-risking through dated milestones with acceptance tests, IP management for a kernel with University of Toronto lineage and patents held [by Uing Technologies — confirm], and venture-building support for a founder whose prior ventures were consumer-facing and whose current one sells evidence to engineering, quality and regulatory teams. The company's decision rule — pre-register the comparison, preserve negative results, prefer the simpler method that achieves the customer outcome — is the same one I use in research, and it is the reason the milestones in the proposal are stated as falsifiers rather than promises.

Disclosure. I am separately applying for postdoctoral awards to be held at the University of Toronto under Prof. Grüninger from April 2027 at the earliest. Any university work on the kernel will be under a written research agreement with the company; no cost will be claimed twice; and the arrangement will be disclosed to TIAP, to the University's Innovations & Partnerships Office and to any co-funder [confirm the arrangement before submission].
