# Timeline and start-up outline — U of T MIE tenure-stream application

No budget is submitted with a faculty application. Two things are needed instead: (A) the application timeline, and (B) a start-up outline the applicant can state at the interview and negotiate at offer. Dollar figures are deliberately absent — U of T start-up packages are negotiated with the Chair and Dean and vary by area; the registry notes NSERC Discovery and CFI JELF eligibility on appointment.
