## Programme in one paragraph

A two-year (24-month) fellowship for early-career researchers from outside the UK to carry out a research project at a UK host organisation, worth **up to GBP 280,000 over two years**, covering salary at the host's scale with on-costs, research expenses, and relocation and visa costs including dependents (registry record, from the 2026 scheme notes). Optional alumni follow-on funding supports continued UK collaboration after the fellowship [amount and duration — confirm in the 2027 scheme notes]. The fellowship must be held entirely in the UK: it is an **alternative to, not an add-on to, the spring-2027 U of T postdoc** (registry flag). Expected start window for the 2027 round: [late 2027 – early 2028 — confirm; the 2026 round's start window was not captured].

Found by web search on 2026-09-25 (search-result summaries only; the pages were not fetched — re-read them when the 2027 scheme notes appear):

- **Scheme page:** https://royalsociety.org/grants/newton-international/ — two years, up to GBP 280,000; ≤ 5 years postdoctoral experience; must live and work outside the UK; not a UK citizen.
- **2026 scheme notes (ref. CH 12/25):** https://www.royalsociety.org/-/media/grants/newton-fellowships/newton-international-fellowships-scheme-notes.pdf — application via **Flexi-Grant**; all references and supporting statements submitted in Flexi-Grant, **in English**; referees and supporting participants recommended to complete **at least 5 working days before the deadline**; the **UK host organisation approver must approve by the deadline** (2026: Wednesday 11 March 2026); the **Head of Department statement** must summarise the organisation's commitment to the candidate and agree to the "Commitments from the Host Organisation" section.
- **2026 FAQ:** https://royalsociety.org/-/media/grants/newton-fellowships/newton-international-fellowship-faq.pdf — funds cover basic salary at the host's scale with on-costs, relocation and visa costs for the fellow and dependents. [Some third-party summaries quote GBP 30,000 p.a. subsistence + up to GBP 8,000 p.a. research expenses + GBP 3,500 relocation; these look like older-round figures — treat the 2027 scheme notes as authoritative.]
- **Manchester scheme page:** https://www.se.manchester.ac.uk/research/fellowships/royal-society-newton-international-fellowship/ — **research proposal max. 1,500 words**: explain why you chose the subject area, what you find particularly important, potential impact or wider benefits to society, and any fieldwork outside the UK (location, duration, justification); **Statement of UK sponsor support: max. 1 page**, on suitability for the project and intentions for career development.
- **British Academy scheme page (humanities/social-science stream; same rules):** https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/funding/schemes/newton-international-fellowships/ and its FAQ.
- **Oxford Physics summary:** https://www.physics.ox.ac.uk/research/research-funding-support/fellowship-applications/postdoctoral-level-fellowships/royal-1.
- **Assessment process (2026 scheme notes):** after eligibility checks, **Part A** is reviewed by two committee members with the closest expertise for longlisting; longlisted **Parts A and B** are reviewed for shortlisting; shortlisted applications are discussed at a committee meeting that recommends funding. **Criteria and weights are not published in the search summaries** [confirm in the 2027 scheme notes]; by precedent the committee assesses (i) the applicant's track record and potential, (ii) the quality and feasibility of the proposed research, (iii) the suitability of the host and sponsor and the mutual benefit, and (iv) the prospect of long-term links between the UK and the applicant's home research base.
- Contact for scheme questions: info@newtonfellowships.org (Manchester page).
