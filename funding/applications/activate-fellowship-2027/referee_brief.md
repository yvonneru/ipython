Activate is a non-profit that funds scientists and engineers to found hard-tech companies. ## What reviewers weigh

Activate selects fellows, not only technologies: the person's ability to lead the commercial development of a scientifically novel technology, full-time, for two years. The most useful reference gives one or two concrete examples — a result you saw me obtain, a system you saw me ship, a decision you saw me make under uncertainty — and states plainly where I stand relative to other early-stage founders or researchers you have known.
