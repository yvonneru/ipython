## C. Relation to other funding

- Activate is equity-free and is a personal fellowship to the founder; it can be held alongside company-side non-dilutive funding (NRC IRAP if Canadian-incorporated; SBIR/STTR if >50% U.S.-owned — the domicile decision determines which; see the registry's company bundle).
- It cannot be held alongside a full-time postdoctoral award (NSERC CPRA, Vector DPF, DSI, CIRTA); one track must be declined before the start of the other.
- Non-governmental funding raised to date must remain below USD 2M at the application deadline [confirm figure]. A priced round before mid-2027 that crosses USD 2M would break eligibility; sequence any raise accordingly.
- Stipend: USD 100,000/yr to the fellow [confirm tax treatment and whether the fellow may also draw salary from the company].
