## Program

Activate is a non-profit that funds scientists and engineers to turn their research into hard-tech companies. The two-year fellowship gives each fellow a USD 100,000 per year living stipend, USD 100,000 in R&D / company support, health benefits, access to a host lab (in Boston, Activate arranges host-lab access with its Harvard/MIT partners), and a mentor and investor network — equity-free (registry; opportunitiesforyouth.org summary; activate.org/the-fellowship). Cohort 2027 starts mid-2027. The Anywhere cohort lets fellows complete the fellowship where they live, is meant for founders who already control a place to run experiments, and adds roughly quarterly in-person meetings plus Activate-sponsored quarterly U.S. trips (activate.org/faq).

This package positions AXIOMALITY — the verification and evidence layer for robot-learning data (offline Engine → data Passport, on a four-layer ontology kernel aligned with ISO/IEC 21838-4:2023) — as a physical-AI infrastructure company founded by the scientist who co-wrote the standard it is built on. It is written from the company brief and the September 2026 decks; the founder's academic applications (NSERC CPRA, Vector, DSI, CIRTA) are separate and appear here only where a conflict must be disclosed or a choice made.
