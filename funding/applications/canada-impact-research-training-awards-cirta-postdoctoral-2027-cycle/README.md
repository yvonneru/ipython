## Program

Tri-agency (NSERC / CIHR / SSHRC) postdoctoral award in the Canada Research Training Awards Suite, launched 2025-26. **Nomination-only:** Canadian universities receive allocations and nominate prospective postdocs who are currently outside Canada; the candidate does not apply directly. **CAD 70,000 per year for 2 years.** Priority research area for this nomination: **Advanced digital technologies (AI)**. Official page: https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/canada-impact-research-training-awards-qa

The program is the second route to the same award value as the CPRA (package already drafted; NSERC deadline 17 Oct 2026). The two cannot be held together. Because the CPRA result (31 Mar 2027) is expected before the likely CIRTA nomination window (spring 2027), the decision to proceed with CIRTA can be made after the CPRA result is known; the nomination package should nevertheless be ready in January 2027 in case U of T runs an early wave.
