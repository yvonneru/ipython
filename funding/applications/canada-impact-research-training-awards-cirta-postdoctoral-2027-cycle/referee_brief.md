## The proposed program in three sentences

Robot-learning datasets (PartNet, PartNet-Mobility, GAPartNet, PartNet-Ensembled, AgiBot World) annotate objects as hierarchies of parts, but no dataset or learned model is required to satisfy the axioms of parthood, and nobody has checked them against any formal specification. The program (1) axiomatizes and verifies an ontology of physical object parts as an extension of ISO/IEC 21838-4 (TUpper) integrated with the Process Specification Language; (2) audits the major datasets against it and derives provably meaning-preserving cross-dataset mappings; and (3) uses the ontology as an inductive bias and an evaluation signal for part-aware manipulation policies in simulation. Outputs: a verified ontology contributed to COLORE and ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42; the first quantitative audit of parthood consistency in robot datasets, released publicly; and measured effects on policy generalization.

Prof. Grüninger's NSERC programs ask which mereotopologies the static part decompositions of ShapeNet and PartNet presuppose; mine adds articulated, functional and assembly parthood, parthood change under manipulation, real-robot corpora, dataset-scale audit and cross-dataset alignment.
