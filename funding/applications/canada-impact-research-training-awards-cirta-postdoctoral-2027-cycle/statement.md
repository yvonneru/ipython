## Part A — Candidate statement (first person; about 600 words)

**Research experience.** I completed my PhD in Information Engineering at the University of Toronto in March 2025 [confirm the requirements-met date], following a BASc in Industrial Engineering at the same university. My doctoral work sat at the intersection of formal ontology, knowledge-system architecture and machine-learning-based simulation, supervised by [name]: it developed an architecture for AI knowledge systems in which a verified formal ontology governs the data model, the learned models and the simulation layer, and elements of it contributed to ISO/IEC 21838-4:2023, the international standard for the TUpper top-level ontology, on which I was a core contributor within ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 [confirm role wording]. With Prof. Michael Grüninger I began the line of work on mereology and material constitution that has now produced two first-author journal submissions to Synthese (2026): a theory of material constitution as a parthood-preserving mapping between mereologies, and a validation of mereological pluralism. Since [month year] I have been a postdoctoral researcher at Harvard Medical School, in [laboratory/department], working on [one sentence]. That appointment has given me a second research environment, a different data regime and an applied community that judges formal methods by whether they work; it has also confirmed for me that the open problem I most want to work on is the one described in the research plan, and that the right place to do it is Toronto.

**From theory to systems.** Before and during my doctorate I founded or co-founded two technology companies. As a founding team member of MICAS I designed and shipped production CRM/ERP and influencer-allocation systems; as co-founder and CEO of Uing Technologies I led the construction of ontology-based representations of objects, indoor environments and spatial relationships for embodied AI, and launched one of the first AR interaction apps on the Apple Vision Pro App Store. I am first inventor on [number] patents [reconcile counts: 13 granted and 9 applications per the company deck; 3 Chinese invention patents and 9–10 German utility patents per the earlier drafts]. The significance for the proposed program is that the ontology-first architecture has been shown to work outside the laboratory, at production scale, with real data-integration problems — the same class of problem, at the same scale, that the audit objective addresses for robot datasets.

**Why this program, and why Toronto.** The proposed research requires three things to be in one place: a verified-ontology methodology with tool support (the Semantic Technologies Laboratory at MIE), a robotics community generating and consuming part-level data (the University of Toronto Robotics Institute and the Vector Institute), and a researcher who has done both formal theory and production data integration. The first two exist only in Toronto; the third is what I bring. I left Toronto after my doctorate; I return not for continuity but for a specific capability, and I do so with a research program that is my own: none of its datasets, methods or experiments appears in my thesis or in the nominator's Discovery Grant programs, while it reuses their verification methodology. The two Synthese papers provide the theory; the physical-AI datasets released over the past three years provide, for the first time, data at a scale where the theory can be tested; and the award's two-year term matches the Y1–Y2 sequence in the plan.

**Training and contribution to the laboratory.** I will co-supervise [number] graduate students on the ontology and audit components, represent the laboratory at ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 meetings, and release all axioms, mappings, code and corrected annotations openly so that groups without access to proprietary robot data can build on the work. I was a teaching assistant in engineering economics, database systems, knowledge modelling and resource and production modelling at U of T, and served as Vice-President of the MIE graduate students' association, MIE representative on the Graduate Student Union and a CUPE 3902 steward.

**Long-term goal.** A faculty position in Canada in knowledge representation for engineering systems. This award is the step that goal requires: an independent program, executed in the one laboratory that can support it, with outputs — a standard contribution, public data, a toolkit — that a hiring committee can inspect.
