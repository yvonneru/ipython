Dear Prof. [name],

Prof. Michael Grüninger suggested I write to you [or: I am writing on the strength of your group's work on [topic]]. I am a postdoctoral researcher at Harvard Medical School with a PhD in Information Engineering from the University of Toronto (2025), and I am preparing an application to the Vector Institute's Distinguished Postdoctoral Fellowship for the Spring 2027 cohort. The fellowship requires a Vector faculty sponsor, and I would like to ask whether you would consider it.

The program in one paragraph: robot manipulation policies are trained on part-level datasets — PartNet, PartNet-Mobility, GAPartNet, AgiBot World — whose annotations carry no formal semantics, so nothing requires the parts a model infers to form a consistent whole and nothing relates the part vocabularies of different datasets. With Prof. Grüninger I have a machine-checkable theory of when two part decompositions of one object are compatible (two papers under review at Synthese), and I helped write ISO/IEC 21838-4, the top-level ontology it extends. I propose to (1) verify an ontology of object parts, (2) audit those datasets against it — the first quantitative measurement of parthood consistency in robot data, with corrected annotations and a merged corpus released publicly — and (3) compile the axioms into differentiable losses and constraint-guided augmentation for part-aware policies, evaluated with an ontology-violation rate reported alongside task success. Part (3) is where I would want to work with your group: the policy backbone, the simulation set-up and the experimental design.

What I would ask of a sponsor: agreement to be named on the application; a short [letter of support — confirm whether Vector requires one]; a home for the Theme 3 experiments; and roughly monthly meetings. What the group gets: an audit of the datasets it trains on, a verified ontology and toolkit it can use, co-authorship on the audit and methods papers, and a fellow who has shipped production data systems. Prof. Grüninger would be co-sponsor for the ontology and audit components.

I attach the research statement and my CV. Could we speak for twenty minutes in the next few weeks? I am in Boston but can meet online at any time, or in Toronto on [dates].
