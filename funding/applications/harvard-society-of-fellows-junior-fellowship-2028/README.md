## Format and criteria (verify on the official page)
Found 2026-09-25 by web search (three searches; WebFetch was unavailable, so the quotations come from search-engine summaries of the official pages and should be re-read on the pages themselves):
- **Nomination form (submitted by the nominator only, online)** — must include: "a letter of nomination [with] a thorough assessment of the candidate's work and promise, i.e. a full letter of recommendation, ideally addressing how this candidate ranks in comparison with other outstanding graduate students known by the nominator"; the candidate's complete contact information including current residential address and email; "the names, institutions, and email addresses of three additional people who agree to write letters of recommendation". Source: https://socfell.fas.harvard.edu/nomination-procedure **(confirmed)**. No page/character limit for the nomination letter was found [check the portal].
- **Candidate materials (requested by the Society after the nomination, due within four weeks)** — "samples of independent work (e.g. dissertation chapters, articles, papers, musical compositions, art work) along with a one or two-page proposal describing the studies they would like to pursue while a Junior Fellow". Source: same page **(confirmed)**. **Proposal limit: 1–2 pages.** No prescribed headings, font or margin were found [assume 12 pt, 1-inch margins; check the Society's email when it arrives]. No CV requirement was found on the page [the registry lists a CV; assume one is requested with the samples — prepare it].
- **Referee letters (three, requested by the Society directly, due within four weeks)** — "the nomination letter is the most important letter in a candidate's file" **(confirmed, FAQ)**; no length guidance found.
- **Review criteria** — no published rubric or weights. The stated standard is "highest caliber of intellectual achievement ... extraordinary creativity and the capacity to shape the future direction of their chosen fields" and "resourcefulness, initiative, and intellectual curiosity ... work [that] holds exceptional promise" **(confirmed wording, About page and nomination-procedure page)**. Reviewers are the Senior Fellows, across all fields; the file must be legible to a historian and a mathematician alike.
- **Not a component**: budget; institutional signature; company documents; personal statement (statement.md is background for the nominator/referees and a cover note for the samples, not a required upload — say so if the Society's email lists only samples and proposal).
