## A. Cover note — Dr. Ru to Prof. Grüninger (send with email 1)

The Alliance International Collaboration grant is the one program on my list where you, not I, would be the applicant, and I want to set out why I think it is worth your time.

What it is. Up to CAD 100,000 a year for three years to a Canadian university researcher for a defined joint project with an academic researcher at a foreign university. No industry partner and no Canadian cash match. The condition that decides everything: the grant funds the Canadian component of a project that the foreign collaborator's own national agency has *just* funded — the collaborator's award letter must be dated no earlier than 120 days before our submission, and a copy of their proposal and award letter go into the NSERC application [Form 101 instructions, from search summaries; verify on the page]. An existing, older grant does not qualify. Cost-sharing is 1:1: our annual request cannot exceed the collaborator's award, converted to CAD. The stream re-opened on 2 September as a competition with three deadlines a year; the first is 25 November 2026, 8 pm ET. The proposal is three pages under five fixed headings (relevance and expected outcomes, collaboration, training, EDI, budget); NSERC evaluates on impact and benefit to Canada (60%) and quality of training in Canada (40%, including EDI).

What it would do for the laboratory. It would pay my postdoctoral salary in MIE from [June 2027], with the remainder for short exchange visits and compute — a MASc stipend fits only if the salary line leaves room, which at CAD 70,000 plus benefits it probably does not; it would formalize a link with [my Harvard supervisor's group], which brings [named datasets / a robot platform / a second annotation domain] that the laboratory does not have; and it would put the parts ontology, the audit of the robot datasets and the contribution back to ISO/IEC 21838-4 on a three-year footing rather than the two years of the CPRA. It does not fund a PhD student on top of the postdoc: CAD 100,000 does not stretch that far, so the draft lists PhD1 and the undergraduates as [Discovery Grant / USRA]-supported. The program is the one you know from my CPRA outline: ontology as specification, as audit, and as inductive bias for part-aware manipulation policies. The Alliance version adds the Harvard side to Objectives 2 and 3 and assigns each open research question to a trainee, as your Discovery proposals do.

What I need from you. (1) A decision by 10 October on whether you will lead it; if you have other priorities this autumn (the RAC, CIRTA), a "no" is the right answer and I will not press. (2) Whether to add a Robotics Institute co-applicant. (3) The research subject code. (4) Your CCV and a sensitive-technology attestation by early November; I will prepare everything else and work with the MIE research office on the internal deadline. I have drafted the three-page proposal in your voice so that you can edit rather than write; nothing in it commits you to a claim you have not made in your own proposals.

What Harvard has told me. I asked the gating question first so as not to spend your time on a call we cannot enter: [my supervisor] [has / does not have] a national-agency award (NSF, NIH, DoE, DoD or similar) for a project this work can join, with an award letter dated on or after 28 July 2026 (120 days before 25 November) and in hand by 30 October [edit to the answer actually received: agency, title, date, annual amount]. If they do not, the stream does not apply this round and we should look at the next deadline [date — confirm] instead. If they do: their agreement, the international proposal and award letter, a letter of support and a biographical sketch are due by 30 October.

One disclosure. AXIOMALITY, the company I founded [in 2026 — confirm the legal entity and its relationship to Uing Technologies], commercializes verification of robotics data with the same family of methods. It is not a party to this grant and would receive no funds or rights; I will disclose it to the research office and in the application if there is a field for it.
