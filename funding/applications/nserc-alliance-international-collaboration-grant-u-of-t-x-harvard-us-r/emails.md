NSERC re-opened the Alliance International Collaboration stream on 2 September as a competition with three deadlines a year; the first is 25 November 2026, 8 pm ET. There is no industry partner and no cash match on our side. The condition that matters: the grant funds the Canadian component of a project the foreign collaborator's own national agency has newly funded — their award letter must be dated within 120 days before our submission, and their proposal and award letter go into our application. I asked that question first: [my Harvard supervisor] has [an award from agency, title, dated date — inside the 120-day window] and is willing to be the international collaborator [edit to the answer actually received]. The proposal is three pages under five fixed headings, evaluated 60% on impact and benefit to Canada and 40% on training in Canada.

The project would be the one in my CPRA outline — a verified parts ontology as a TUpper extension with PSL, the audit of the part-level robot datasets, and the ontology as a consistency layer on manipulation policies — with [my Harvard supervisor's group] as the international side, contributing [datasets / a robot platform / a second domain for the audit toolkit] and hosting exchange visits. The grant would pay my postdoctoral salary in MIE from [June 2027], plus travel and compute; a MASc stipend fits only if the salary line leaves room, and a PhD student and undergraduates would need [Discovery Grant / USRA] support. If both CPRA and this succeed, the salary moves to CPRA and this grant funds students, travel and compute.

I have drafted the three-page proposal in your voice, the budget, and the collaborator's letter, so that your part is editing, your CCV, and the sensitive-technology attestation. I attach the one-page brief and the draft.

Three questions:
1. Will you lead it? I would need a decision by 10 October so that I can start the MIE research office process; if the RAC and CIRTA already fill your autumn, a no is the right answer.
2. Would you add a Robotics Institute co-applicant, and if so whom?
3. Which research subject code — Computing Sciences or Civil, Industrial and Systems Engineering?

One disclosure, which I will also make to the research office: AXIOMALITY, the company I founded [this year — confirm entity and date], commercializes verification of robotics data with the same family of methods. It would not be a party to the grant and would receive no funds or rights.
