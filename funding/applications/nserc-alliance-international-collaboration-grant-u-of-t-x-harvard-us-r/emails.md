## 1. To Prof. Michael Grüninger — send now (can be combined with the CPRA thread)

**Subject:** NSERC Alliance International Collaboration grant (deadline Nov 25) — would you lead a U of T–Harvard application with me as the postdoc?

Dear Michael,

Following on from the CPRA application: there is a second NSERC route to the same program that would have you as the applicant rather than me, and I would like to ask whether you are willing to lead it.

NSERC re-opened the Alliance International Collaboration stream on 2 September as a competition with three deadlines a year; the first is 25 November 2026, 8 pm ET. It gives a Canadian university researcher up to CAD 100,000 a year for three years for a defined joint project with an academic researcher at a foreign university. There is no industry partner and no cash match on our side; the condition is that the foreign collaborator's own funding for the joint work is at least equal to the NSERC request. The proposal is three pages under five fixed headings, and fundable applications are ranked 60% on benefit to Canada and 40% on training in Canada.

The project would be the one in my CPRA outline — a verified parts ontology as a TUpper extension with PSL, the audit of the part-level robot datasets, and the ontology as a consistency layer on manipulation policies — with [my Harvard supervisor's group] as the international side, contributing [datasets / a robot platform / a second domain for the audit toolkit] and hosting exchange visits. The grant would pay my postdoctoral salary in MIE from spring 2027 and support a graduate student. If both CPRA and this succeed, the salary moves to CPRA and this grant funds students, travel and compute.

I have drafted the three-page proposal in your voice, the budget, and the collaborator's letter, so that your part is editing, your CCV, and the sensitive-technology attestation. I attach the one-page brief and the draft.

Three questions:
1. Will you lead it? I would need a decision by 2 October so that I can ask Harvard and start the MIE research office process.
2. Would you add a Robotics Institute co-applicant, and if so whom?
3. Which research subject code — Computing Sciences or Civil, Industrial and Systems Engineering?

One disclosure, which I will also make to the research office: AXIOMALITY, the company I founded this year, commercializes verification of robotics data with the same family of methods. It would not be a party to the grant and would receive no funds or rights.

Best,
Yi
