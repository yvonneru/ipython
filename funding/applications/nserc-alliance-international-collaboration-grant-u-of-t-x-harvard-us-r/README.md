## Program

NSERC Alliance International **Collaboration** grants: support for a Canadian university researcher to carry out a defined joint project with an academic researcher at a foreign university or public research institution. Registry-verified amount: **up to CAD 100,000 per year for up to three years** (ceiling confirmed; a CAD 20,000 floor was not re-verified). No industry partner and no cash match from the Canadian side; **but the international collaborator must hold funding of their own for the joint work at least equal to the amount requested from NSERC** (Form 101 instructions — see "Format and criteria"). Official page: https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/alliance-international-collaboration-grants

Why this program for Dr. Ru: a Collaboration grant can pay Dr. Ru's Toronto postdoctoral salary from spring 2027 while formalizing the Toronto–Harvard link, with no personal eligibility barrier (no citizenship or years-since-PhD rule for HQP). The two obstacles are that (i) Prof. Grüninger must agree to lead, and (ii) a foreign academic collaborator with their own funding must commit within weeks.

## Format and criteria (verify on the official page)

Found by web search on 2026-09-25 (three searches; WebFetch blocked, so figures below are from search summaries of the official pages and must be checked against the Form 101 instructions before drafting is finalized):

- **Proposal length: maximum three pages in English** (Form 101 proposal template). The template's own text counts toward the limit and must not be removed; the instructions give suggested lengths per section (about 0.75 page each). Figures and tables are allowed within the limit. Source: https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/alliance-international-collaboration-grants-instructions-completing-application-form-101
- **Proposal sections (five, equally weighted in merit review): Relevance and expected outcomes; Collaboration; Training; Equity, diversity and inclusion; Budget (justification).** The proposal must address every criterion and sub-criterion to be considered fundable. Same source.
- **Ranking of fundable applications: Impact and benefit to Canada 60%; Quality of training in Canada 40%.** Source: https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/alliance-international (also recorded in the registry). Read the two lists together: the five sections decide fundability; the 60/40 weights decide rank.
- **Budget rule:** the budget justification must state the annual support obtained by the international collaborator(s), converted to Canadian dollars; the amount requested depends on that support, and the collaborator's contribution must be at least equal to the NSERC request. Same Form 101 source.
- **Other components:** Form 101 (proposal, budget, HQP table) in the NSERC online system; Form 100A personal data form with CCV attachment for the applicant and any co-applicant; a **biographical sketch for each international collaborator** named in the international proposal; **Attestation for Research Aiming to Advance Sensitive Technology Research Areas** for the applicant, co-applicants and all named collaborators; [letter of support from the international collaborator — expected by the registry; confirm whether Form 101 has an upload or whether it is included with the biographical sketch]. Sources: Form 101 instructions page above; https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/alliance-international-catalyst-grants-and-collaboration-grants
- **Timeline and process:** re-opened 2 Sept 2026; competitive process with three deadlines per year; 2026 deadline 25 Nov 2026, 8 pm ET. Sources: https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/alliance-international ; https://www.lakeheadu.ca/research-and-innovation/research-services/funding-prizes/funding-db/node/351436 ; pause notice https://www.uoguelph.ca/research/alerts/content/nserc-alliance-international-catalyst-and-collaboration-grants-program-paused
- **Not found by search (verify):** whether the reference list counts toward the three pages; font and margin rules (assume NSERC's standard on-line presentation standards: 12 pt, 0.75 in margins); whether a public summary field exists; whether the collaborator letter is uploaded separately; expected decision date.
