## Program

NSERC Alliance International **Collaboration** grants: support for a Canadian university researcher to carry out a defined joint project with an academic researcher at a foreign university or public research institution. Registry-verified amount: **up to CAD 100,000 per year for up to three years** (ceiling confirmed; a CAD 20,000 floor was not re-verified). No industry partner and no cash match from the Canadian side; **but the international collaborator must hold national-agency funding for the joint project at least equal to the amount requested from NSERC (1:1 cost-sharing), newly awarded (NSERC application no later than 120 days after the international award letter), and the international proposal and award letter are attached to the application** (see "Format and criteria"). Official page: https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/alliance-international-collaboration-grants

Why this program for Dr. Ru: a Collaboration grant can pay Dr. Ru's Toronto postdoctoral salary from spring 2027 while formalizing the Toronto–Harvard link, with no personal eligibility barrier (no citizenship or years-since-PhD rule for HQP). The two obstacles are that (i) a foreign academic collaborator with a *newly awarded* national-agency grant must commit within two weeks, and (ii) Prof. Grüninger must agree to lead. See the gate above.
