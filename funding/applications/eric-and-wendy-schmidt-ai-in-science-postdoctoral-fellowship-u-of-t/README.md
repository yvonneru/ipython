## Program in one paragraph
Schmidt Sciences funds a global network of "AI in Science" postdoctoral fellowships; the University of Toronto node is administered by the Data Sciences Institute (DSI) with the Acceleration Consortium. The fellowship pays CAD 85,000/yr salary plus about CAD 11,000/yr benefits/levy for two years, with a start date between 1 May 2027 and 1 January 2028, and enrols fellows in a cohort AI-training programme. The fellow must hold a PhD in the natural sciences or engineering, must have a U of T faculty supervisor (any campus), and must propose a project that applies AI methods to the fellow's own scientific or engineering domain. Prior AI expertise is not required; a demonstrated desire to learn AI methods is expected. Domestic and international candidates are eligible. Official page: https://datasciences.utoronto.ca/schmidt-fellows/
