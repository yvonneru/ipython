## Program in one paragraph
Schmidt Sciences funds a global network of "AI in Science" postdoctoral fellowships; the University of Toronto node is co-led by the Data Sciences Institute (DSI) with the Acceleration Consortium. The fellowship pays CAD 85,000/yr salary plus a fixed CAD 11,000/yr towards the benefit rate and postdoctoral levy incurred by the supervisor's unit, for two years, with a start date between 1 May 2027 and 1 January 2028 [window from registry — verify], and enrols fellows in a cohort AI-training programme. It is for people who have completed a PhD in the natural sciences or engineering and aim to pursue a research project that applies AI to their domain. Applicants must demonstrate clear expertise and promise within their field; experience with AI is not a requirement, but applicants must have a desire to learn new AI methodologies that stand to accelerate discoveries in their domain. Domestic and international candidates are eligible [registry]. Official page: https://datasciences.utoronto.ca/schmidt-fellows/ ; apply page reported by search: https://schmidtfellows.utoronto.ca/apply/ [verify this is the AI in Science apply page, not the SGS "Schmidt Science Fellows" nomination page, which is a different programme].
