**Subject:** Co-supervision request — Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellowship (deadline Oct 5)

Dear Prof. [name],

Prof. Michael Grüninger (MIE) suggested I write to you. I am applying for the Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellowship at U of T, due 5 October, with Michael as supervisor, and I am looking for a co-supervisor on the machine-learning side.

The project: robot-manipulation datasets (PartNet, PartNet-Mobility, GAPartNet, AgiBot World and others) annotate objects as part hierarchies with no formal semantics and no way to map labels across datasets. I will build a verified ontology of object parts (an extension of ISO/IEC 21838-4, which I helped write), audit and align those datasets against it, and then use the ontology as an inductive bias — constraint losses, constraint-guided augmentation and a verification-in-the-loop evaluation that reports ontology-violation rate alongside task success — for part-aware manipulation policies in SAPIEN, measuring generalization to unseen object categories. The third part is where I need a group that trains and evaluates manipulation policies, and where the fellowship's training programme is meant to bring me up to speed in deep robot learning.

What I would ask of you: agreement to co-supervise; a short paragraph for (or co-signature of) the supervisor letter by [date] [verify whether DSI accepts two supervisors]; access to a part-aware manipulation pipeline and simulation environment; and roughly monthly meetings. What you would get: a verified, released parts ontology and audit toolkit usable in your own work; a merged, ontology-aligned corpus; co-authorship on the audit paper and the methods papers; and a fellow who has built production data systems and shipped a 3D spatial application.

The two-page proposal and my statement of training needs are attached. I would be glad to meet this week, and I will of course understand if the timing is too tight.

Best regards,
