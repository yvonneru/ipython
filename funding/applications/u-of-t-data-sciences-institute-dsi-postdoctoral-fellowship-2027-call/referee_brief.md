## The proposed program in three sentences

Robot-learning datasets (PartNet, PartNet-Mobility, GAPartNet, AgiBot World) annotate objects as hierarchies of parts, but no dataset or learned model is required to satisfy the axioms of parthood, and nobody has checked them against any formal specification. The program (1) axiomatizes and verifies an ontology of physical object parts as an extension of ISO/IEC 21838-4 (TUpper) integrated with the Process Specification Language; (2) develops a general, scalable method for auditing hierarchical annotations against a formal specification, applies it to the major datasets, and derives cross-dataset mappings that are meaning-preserving by proof; and (3) measures, with a statistical evaluation design, whether ontology-aligned data pools without loss and whether ontology constraints improve a manipulation policy's generalization to unseen object categories. Outputs: a verified ontology contributed to COLORE and ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42; the first quantitative audit of parthood consistency in robot datasets, released publicly with a reusable toolkit; and measured effects on policy generalization.
