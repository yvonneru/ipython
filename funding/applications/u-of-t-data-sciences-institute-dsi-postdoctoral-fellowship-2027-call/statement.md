Postdoctoral Fellowship Committee, Data Sciences Institute, University of Toronto

Dear Committee,

I am applying to the Data Sciences Institute Postdoctoral Fellowship (2027 call) with Prof. Michael Grüninger (Mechanical and Industrial Engineering) and Prof. [name] ([Statistical Sciences / Computer Science / Robotics Institute]) as co-supervisors. My research is on knowledge-grounded representation for physical AI: I build formally verified ontologies of physical objects and their parts and use them as specifications, audits and inductive biases for the datasets and learned policies that robot manipulation is built on.

The proposal attached is a data-science project. Physical AI is trained on a handful of large part-level datasets whose annotation schemas were designed independently, are pooled freely, and have never been checked against any formal specification of what a "part" is. I propose a general method for auditing hierarchical annotations against a verified formal specification, apply it to produce the first quantitative measurement of parthood consistency in robot datasets, derive cross-dataset label mappings that are meaning-preserving by proof, release a merged ontology-aligned corpus and a reusable toolkit, and measure, with a statistical evaluation design, whether alignment and constraints change what policies learn. The method applies beyond robotics — to part labels in medical imaging, CAD assemblies and building information models — which is why the project needs two disciplines and why I am bringing it to DSI.
