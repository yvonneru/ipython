## Program in one paragraph
The Data Sciences Institute (DSI) at the University of Toronto funds postdoctoral fellows to carry out multidisciplinary or interdisciplinary data-science research under two U of T co-supervisors from complementary disciplines, at least one of whom has a track record in developing novel statistical or computational tools. Value CAD 60,000 per year plus benefits (registry figure), for up to two years, starting between 1 May and 1 December of the award year. The call is annual (deadline in January), with an information session historically in early December, and the co-supervisors' structured forms are submitted about a week after the applicant deadline. Official page: https://datasciences.utoronto.ca/postdoctoral-fellowship/

- Project requirement, in DSI's words: "a research project that has a data sciences focus, through the development of novel methodology or the innovative application of existing approaches."
