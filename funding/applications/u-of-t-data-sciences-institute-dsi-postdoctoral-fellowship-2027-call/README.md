## Program in one paragraph
The Data Sciences Institute (DSI) at the University of Toronto funds postdoctoral fellows to carry out multidisciplinary or interdisciplinary data-science research under two U of T co-supervisors from complementary disciplines, at least one of whom has a track record in developing statistical or computational methods. Value CAD 60,000 per year plus benefits, for up to two years. The call is annual (January), with an information session historically in early December, and the co-supervisors' letters are submitted about a week after the applicant deadline. Official page: https://datasciences.utoronto.ca/postdoctoral-fellowship/

## What the review pass confirmed from the DSI site (search summaries, 2026-09-25 — re-read the page itself)
- Project requirement, in DSI's words: "a research project that has a data sciences focus, through the development of novel methodology or the innovative application of existing approaches."
- Co-supervisors: "at least two faculty members from complementary disciplinary backgrounds ... who together can supervise the different multidisciplinary aspects"; "at least one supervisor should have a track record supporting the training and development of novel statistical or computational tools"; co-supervisors **may be from the same academic unit** if they represent different disciplines and one is focused on developing novel methodology. A supervisor must hold a tenure or tenure-stream appointment at U of T, or be a PI eligible for tri-council funding at a DSI funding-partner institution.
- Start window: fellowships **begin between 1 May and 1 December of the award year**. PhD completion: international applicants must have completed all PhD requirements before the start date; Canadian citizens/PRs within six months after it. Within 5 years of the PhD defence at start. Open to domestic and international applicants; international awardees must meet Canadian immigration requirements.
