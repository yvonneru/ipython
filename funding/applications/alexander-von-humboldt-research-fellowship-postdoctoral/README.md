## Program in one paragraph
The Alexander von Humboldt Foundation funds researchers from abroad to carry out a self-chosen research project in Germany with an academic host of their choice. The postdoctoral track is open to all nationalities except German, to researchers who completed the doctorate less than four years before the application is received, for a research stay of 6–24 months. There is no fixed deadline: applications are accepted at any time and decided by a selection committee that meets about three times a year (roughly March, July and November). The Foundation selects on the applicant's academic record and on the originality and independence of the research proposal; the host's statement is central to the review. Funder page: https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/humboldt-research-fellowship

## Format and criteria (verify on the official page)
Found via web search on 2026-09-25; the Foundation page and its programme-information PDF are the authorities. Re-check both before submission.
- Research outline: approximately five pages in total including bibliography; it must cover the current state of research, evidenced by about five relevant publications from the field (one page maximum), the aims, the methods, a work and time schedule, and the intended duration of the fellowship and division of visits, and it must be agreed with the host before applying. Source: https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/fileadmin/Bewerben/Programme/Humboldt-Forschungsstipendium/humboldt-fellowship_programme_information.pdf (title "Programme information Humboldt Research Fellowships"), and https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/humboldt-research-fellowship
- Key publications: only publications in journals or with publishers reviewed to international standards, commensurate with career stage after the doctorate; registry says up to five. Applicants who apply right after the doctorate should have the key findings of the thesis already published or accepted. Source: Foundation page.
- Host statement: "a detailed expert's statement by an academic host at a research institution in Germany", plus confirmation that research facilities are available and a mentoring agreement [confirm the mentoring-agreement form]. The host statement is described as crucial to the review, focusing on the applicant's creative potential and the innovative idea underlying the intended research. Source: Foundation page; https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/humboldt-research-fellowship/information-for-hosts
- Expert reviews: two, from important collaborative partners and/or academics from the applicant's own institute and other institutions, if possible also outside the country where the applicant currently works; the doctoral supervisor's review is mandatory (registry). Source: Foundation page.
- Review criteria (published, no numerical weights; re-verified by web search 2026-09-25 on humboldt-foundation.de): applications are peer-reviewed, relative to career level, on (1) academic career and record to date (mobility, determination, thematic scope, academic productivity); (2) quality of the key publications (originality, innovativeness, evaluation of the applicant's own contribution to multi-author publications); (3) originality and innovative potential of the proposed research (significance for the field, convincing choice of methods, potential for scientific development, clear focus and feasibility within the requested period, viability at the host institute); (4) future potential (academic potential, personal development, career prospects). The decision is taken by a selection committee of 60–70 researchers from various fields (an earlier draft of this README said 15–20; that was wrong). The Foundation also states that the applicant must have developed the proposal themselves; the project must not be a direct continuation of the doctoral thesis; returning to the same academic environment generally leads to rejection for lack of independent development. Source: Foundation page.
- Timing: "applications should be submitted at least four to seven months ahead of the prospective selection date." Source: Foundation page.
- Amount: unresolved conflict. Do not quote a figure anywhere until confirmed on the Foundation page.
