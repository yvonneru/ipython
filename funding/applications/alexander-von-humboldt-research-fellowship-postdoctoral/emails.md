Subject: Humboldt Research Fellowship (Germany) — sequencing question and three host introductions

Dear Michael,

Following on from the CPRA application: I am considering a second, complementary route for the same programme, an Alexander von Humboldt Research Fellowship for postdoctoral researchers, which would fund a 12-month stay with a German host. There is no deadline; the committee meets in March, July and November, and an application filed by early November would be decided in March 2027. Eligibility is on record alone (doctorate under four years old, any nationality except German), and the proposal is judged on originality, feasibility at the host and independence, so the mereology work and the TUpper contribution are exactly what the committee reads. Realistically the host statement will not be in hand before mid-December, so the July 2027 committee (submission by mid-January 2027) is the likely target, with March 2027 as the stretch.

Two things I need from you.

First, sequencing. The stay must be physically in Germany and cannot overlap a tri-agency award held at U of T. I see three options: a 12-month German stay before a Toronto appointment (which pushes the Toronto start to 2028), a stay inside the Toronto period as a leave, or a stay after the Toronto award, which runs up against the four-year PhD window in March 2029. I would value your view on which of these MIE could accommodate, and on whether the CPRA reviewers would read a parallel Humboldt application as a strength or a distraction.

Second, hosts. The candidates I have in mind are the Institute for Artificial Intelligence at Bremen (CRC EASE, KnowRob), the Institute of Cognitive Science at Osnabrück, and Computational Logic at TU Dresden, with Fraunhofer IPA and the Munich robotics groups as alternates. A warm introduction from you to whichever of these you know through FOIS, IAOA or SC 32 would be worth more than any cold email of mine. I attach the five-page outline, which is the O1/O2 module of the programme sized to twelve months with the host's knowledge base as the validation instrument.

Separately, the Foundation requires a review from the doctoral supervisor and at least one further reviewer; [if you were my doctoral supervisor: I would ask you for the mandatory review; if not: I would like to ask you for the second review]. The request would come four weeks before submission.

Best,
