The Foundation's postdoctoral application is form-based: there is no free-standing cover letter. The portal asks for short texts on the academic career, the doctoral thesis, the choice of host and the reasons for a stay in Germany, alongside the research outline. ## 1. Academic career and research profile [portal field: confirm name and limit]

I am a knowledge-representation researcher working at the boundary of formal ontology and machine learning for physical AI. I build formally verified first-order ontologies of physical objects and their parts, and I use them as specifications, audits and inductive biases for robot-learning datasets and manipulation policies. I have carried formal methods into practice through an international standard, patents and production systems at companies I founded. My career has been mobile by design: doctorate in Toronto, postdoctoral work at Harvard, and now a German host whose robots are the validation instrument my theory lacks.

I completed a BASc in Industrial Engineering at the University of Toronto in 2015 (President Scholarship; Dean's Honours List) and a PhD in Information Engineering at the same university, with all requirements completed in March 2025 on the Fast Track PhD programme. My doctoral research developed an architecture for AI knowledge systems in which a verified formal ontology governs the data model, the learned models and the simulation layer; elements of it contributed to ISO/IEC 21838-4:2023, the international standard for the TUpper top-level ontology, on which I was a core contributor within ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 32 [confirm role wording]. With Prof. Michael Grüninger (University of Toronto) I have developed a formal theory of material constitution as a parthood-preserving mapping between mereologies and a validation of mereological pluralism; both papers are under review at Synthese (2026). I am first author of both and developed the theory and the proofs [confirm wording of the contribution statement; the Foundation assesses the applicant's own contribution to multi-author publications]. My publication record comprises [21] papers [confirm list and venues], the Distinguished Paper Award at FOIS 2018 [confirm role], an invited AAAI presentation [confirm] and an English-language monograph [confirm title]. I am first inventor on multiple patents in 3D recognition, indoor modelling and automatic reconstruction [reconcile counts].

Alongside the academic work I have founded or co-founded technology companies in which ontology-governed data systems were built and shipped at production scale: YourTable Inc. (2018–2021, recommendation systems; incubated by U of T, Schulich and Imperial College London), the founding team of MICAS (2021–2022, cross-border commerce; led a 100+ person team and built its CRM/ERP systems), Uing Technologies (2022–present, structured 3D physical-world datasets and ontology-based representations for embodied AI; an AR application on the Apple Vision Pro App Store) and AXIOMALITY (2026, robotics data verification). ## 3. Choice of host and reasons for a research stay in Germany [portal field: confirm]

I have chosen Prof. [name] at [institute, university] because the project needs, in one place, a deployed knowledge base that represents object parts for real robots, a group that generates and consumes part-level robot data, and a community in which formal ontology is practised rather than tolerated. [The host's group / the CRC EASE programme and KnowRob — confirm] is among the most complete such environments. The verification methodology I use was developed in Toronto and I bring it with me; what I cannot bring is the validation instrument, and that is what the host provides: a knowledge base against which the parts ontology can be checked (Project 1.2), a body of real robot data to audit beyond the public datasets (Project 2.2), and a platform for the first policy experiment (Project 3.1). The host receives, in return, a verified ontology aligned with their own representations, an audit of the datasets their systems train on, an audit toolkit, and co-authorship on the two planned papers.

Germany also hosts a strong applied-ontology community and the DIN mirror committee to ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 32 [confirm] into which the ontology will feed, and it is a jurisdiction in which the EU AI Act's Annex I obligations for AI in machinery and robots will first be enforced from August 2028; machine-checkable evidence about training data is precisely what those obligations will require. I intend the stay to found a lasting collaboration: joint supervision of the Theme 3 follow-on experiments, a joint proposal after the stay [confirm instrument], and a standing exchange between the host's laboratory and [the Semantic Technologies Laboratory in Toronto — per the sequencing decision; rewrite once the order of the German stay and any Toronto appointment is fixed].
