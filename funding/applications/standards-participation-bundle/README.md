### ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 — Artificial intelligence (via a national mirror committee)
- **What.** The ISO/IEC joint subcommittee responsible for both the ISO/IEC 21838 top-level-ontology series (Part 4 is TUpper, published 2023) and the ISO/IEC 5259 data-quality-for-analytics-and-ML series (registry: Parts 1–5 published 2024–2025, so the near-term opening is a maintenance/extension contribution or a new-work-item proposal, not base drafting). - **How experts get in.** Only through a national member body's mirror committee. For Canada that is the Standards Council of Canada (SCC); for the US it is INCITS/AI, the ANSI-accredited US TAG, which registers experts through an INCITS member *organisation* (small-company dues on the order of USD 1–3k/yr [confirm current schedule]). Dr. Ru contributed to 21838-4, so was registered by some national body — [most likely SCC while at U of T — confirm]; the first check is whether that registration is still active and under which organisation.
- **Cadence.** Plenaries roughly April and October. National-body document cut-offs are typically 6–8 weeks before a plenary, so a contribution for the spring-2027 plenary goes through the mirror committee by ~mid-February 2027 [confirm exact dates with the SCC secretariat]. Work items run 2–4 years.
- **Money.** None. SCC mirror-committee membership is free for individual experts (registry, from scc.ca — [confirm]); plenary travel is self-funded unless SCC delegate support applies (see next record).

### IEEE Ontologies for Robotics and Automation (ORA) Working Group — IEEE 1872 series
- **What.** The IEEE SA working group, sponsored by the IEEE RAS Standing Committee for Standards Activities, behind IEEE 1872-2015 (Core Ontology for Robotics and Automation, CORA — built on the SUMO upper ontology), 1872.1-2017 (robot task representation), 1872.2-2021 (Autonomous Robotics Ontology) and IEEE 7007-2021 (ethically driven robotics ontology). A PAR has a 4-year (extendable) lifetime; PAR-to-standard typically 2–4 years. An entity-process PAR would require AXIOMALITY to be an IEEE SA entity member (fee unverified). ## Why participation matters

### For the research program
- **O1 → ISO/IEC 21838-4 and SC 42.** O1 axiomatises and verifies a modular first-order ontology of physical object parts (rigid, articulated, functional, assembly) *as an extension of TUpper* integrated with PSL, verified with Prover9/Mace4 by the COLORE methodology (research_program.md). The deliverable is stated as "verified parts ontology (open licence; contributed to COLORE and ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42)". SC 42 is the only place that contribution can land: either as input to 21838-4 maintenance [whether a systematic review or revision is scheduled — confirm on the work programme] or as a new work item for a parts-ontology extension aligned with 21838-4 (applications.md §D). The applicant's familiarity with the standard, its editors and SC 42 procedure is what the CPRA drafts already cite as making the dissemination plan realistic.
- **O2 → ISO/IEC 5259.** O2 produces the first quantitative measurement of parthood consistency in robot datasets. The registry's SC 42 record maps this to a "data-quality profile for robot-learning datasets" relating O2's audit metrics to the 5259 measures — a second possible SC 42 contribution (registry next action: 3–4 page outline by 31 Jan 2027).
- **O1 → IEEE ORA alignment.** IEEE 1872 (CORA) is built on SUMO and the ORA community seeks alignment with top-level ontologies (registry). A TUpper-based parts/affordance/kinematic-chain ontology is the natural content for a CORA-family revision or a new 1872.x PAR; the Semantic Technologies Laboratory's CORA–SUMO/COLORE overlap is a specific question for Prof. Grüninger (emails.md §1).
- **IAOA / FOIS-JOWO 2027 → dissemination.** FOIS 2027 is the registry's "single best venue" for the O1 result (verified parts ontology as a TUpper/PSL extension with representation theorems, distinct from the Synthese submissions to avoid double publication) and a JOWO 2027 workshop on ontology-based auditing of robot-learning datasets would convene exactly the O2 audience. The Early Career Symposium and a FOIS award add visibility for the fellowship and faculty files.
