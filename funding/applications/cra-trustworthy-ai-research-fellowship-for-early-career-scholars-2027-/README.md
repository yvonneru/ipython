## Read this first — what the searches changed

The registry entry described this program as a paid postdoctoral fellowship with a US host-mentor letter and recommendation letters. The funder's own pages for the second (2026–27) cohort describe something different, and the package is drafted to what the funder actually published:

- It is a **cohort fellowship, not a salaried postdoc**: a **USD 17,000 stipend** plus travel, lodging and meals for a **four-day in-person Trustworthy AI Field School**, with mentorship from established scholars across computing, the humanistic social sciences, ethics and policy (past mentors reported by an earlier search summary as [Barbara Grosz, Desmond Patton, Moshe Vardi, James Mickens and Bobby Kleinberg — verify every name on the cohort announcement pages before citing any]).
- **Cohort calendar (cohort 2):** June 2026 – August 2027, opening with a virtual kickoff in June and the Field School on **26–31 July 2026 at Microsoft Research, Cambridge, Massachusetts**. Cohort 3 is assumed to run June 2027 – August 2028 [unconfirmed]. Cohort 2 drew **more than 300 eligible applicants**; the program is selective.
- Applicants must already **hold a tenure-track faculty or visiting-researcher position at a US institution of higher education**, and the call's own parenthesis reads **"(e.g., postdoc or fellowship)"**, so a postdoctoral appointment satisfies the position type. They must be **1–3 years post-PhD** (cohort 2: doctorate conferred 1 May 2023 – 1 July 2025), hold a **primary doctorate in a computing-related discipline**, and have **secondary training or substantial research experience in at least one social-science discipline**; the call lists **anthropology, communication and media studies, studies of vulnerable populations, history, labour studies, political science, science and technology studies (STS), and sociology**. Economics and business are not on that list.
- The application is a **brief online form, a CV and short written responses** on (i) interdisciplinary background, (ii) interest in the fellowship, and (iii) experience with or interest in community-based and public-interest approaches to trustworthy AI. The program page asks applicants to "clearly outline how their background aligns with the fellowship's focus". **No letters of recommendation are required.** No research-proposal upload was listed for cohort 2.
- Applications for the 2027–28 cohort are **expected to open in early 2027**. A notification form exists on the program page; contact is Janine Myszka, jmyszka@cra.org.

Consequences for this package: `proposal.md` is a compact research plan that feeds the written responses and mentor conversations (it is not a required upload unless cohort 3 adds one); `statement.md` holds the three short responses; `referee_brief.md` is a supporters' brief, because no referees are needed; `budget_and_timeline.md` is a stipend-use plan and a 12-month milestone timeline.

## Program in one paragraph

A CRA–Microsoft fellowship that brings a small cohort of early-career computing researchers with social-science training together for a year of mentorship, a four-day Field School and a USD 17,000 stipend, to advance interdisciplinary, community-based and public-interest research on trustworthy AI. Held alongside the fellow's existing US faculty or visiting-researcher position. Official page: https://cra.org/cra-trustworthy-ai-research-fellowship/
