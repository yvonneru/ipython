## Program in one paragraph

HDSI's flagship two-year postdoctoral fellowship for researchers "in data science, broadly construed," including researchers with a primarily methodological focus and researchers who advance both methodology and application. Fellows pursue their own research agenda, collaborate with other fellows and with faculty across all Harvard schools, and receive a salary plus an annual allocation for research and travel (2025-26 cycle advertised at about USD 85,000 salary plus about USD 10,000 research/travel per year [confirm on the 2026-27 call]). Applicants identify at least two and up to five Harvard faculty members from across the university with whom they would like to work. Official pages: https://datascience.harvard.edu/programs/fellowships-funds/hdsi-postdoctoral-fellows/ (current program page, found 25 Sept 2026 in the review search; it describes the fellowship as "two to three year" and fellows as "independent investigators"), https://datascience.harvard.edu/?p=2779 (2025-26 call, "normal duration ... two years") and https://datascience.harvard.edu/programs/postdoctoral-fellowship/ (older program page). Contact: datascience@harvard.edu, 617-496-2743.

**Strategic note (decide before investing more than a day).** A 2027–2029 Harvard appointment is incompatible with the spring-2027 return to Toronto planned in the CPRA, Vector, DSI and CIRTA packages. Both tracks can be applied for; only one can be accepted. Decision points: HDSI decisions are expected in January with offers late January–early February [prior cycle]; CPRA results ~31 March 2027. The proposal below is written so that the ontology, audit toolkit and released data carry over to whichever track is chosen.
