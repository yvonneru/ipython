Dr. Yi Ru is a knowledge-representation researcher whose work combines formal ontology with machine learning and has been carried into practice through an international standard, patents and deployed systems. A core contributor to ISO/IEC 21838-4:2023 (the TUpper top-level ontology), Dr. Ru developed, with me, a formal theory of material constitution and mereological pluralism now under review at Synthese, and has founded or co-founded two technology companies in which ontology-governed data systems were built and shipped at production scale. The proposed program — verified mereological ontologies for object–part representation in physical AI — brings the verification methodology of my laboratory to the part-level datasets now driving robot learning, and sits squarely in the AI priority area.
Research plan summary (nominator may paste; matches the NSERC outline). Robot-learning datasets annotate objects as assemblies of parts, but each defines “part” operationally and no dataset or learned model is required to satisfy the axioms of parthood. The program will (1) axiomatize and verify an ontology of physical object parts as a modular extension of ISO/IEC 21838-4 integrated with the Process Specification Language; (2) align the part vocabularies of PartNet, PartNet-Mobility, GAPartNet, PartNet-Ensembled and AgiBot World to it and audit them automatically for parthood violations and cross-dataset inconsistency; and (3) use the verified ontology as a consistency layer on part-aware manipulation policies in simulation and measure the effect on generalization to unseen object categories. Outputs: a verified ontology contributed to the COLORE repository and to ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42; the first quantitative audit of parthood consistency in robot datasets with public release; and policy results. Training environment: Semantic Technologies Laboratory (MIE), with collaborators at the University of Toronto Robotics Institute and the Vector Institute.
