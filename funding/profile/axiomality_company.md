# AXIOMALITY — company brief (from the Sept 2026 US/Canada deck and the Chinese deck)

Tagline: "Engineering the evidence layer" — robotics data verification, certification and exchange mechanism for embodied-AI data.

## What it is
A verification and evaluation layer that every data-generation route (teleoperation, world models, simulation) and every robot deployment can use. Six-stage versioned evidence loop: Collect → Ground → Verify → Augment → Deliver → Learn. First commercial unit: the offline Engine (ingestion → scene graph → SMT check) and the data Passport (reproducible per-release evidence pack; audit pack reviewed with a Big Four accounting firm and an international law firm).

## Technical core
- Four ontology layers: TUpper (ISO/IEC 21838-4) foundational; physical world (parts, shape, measurement, materials, change, process); robotics domain (embodiment, kinematic chains, affordances, contacts, task phases, safety conditions); dataset instances (episodes, frames, sensors).
- Formal checking: Common Logic modules; Prover9 theorem obligations; Mace4 counter-models; compiled SMT/task-checking subset; UNKNOWN/timeout are first-class outputs.
- Physics residuals with an observation contract; conformal calibrated uncertainty controls abstention; adapters for LeRobot, RLDS, ROS 2/MCAP, OpenUSD/USDZ, PLY, JSON.
- Runtime semantic guardrails as a separately gated product.
