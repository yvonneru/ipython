## 0. The verdict

The portfolio has one anchor and one fork. The anchor is the **NSERC CPRA (17 Oct 2026, 8 pm ET)**: highest fit (92–95), CAD 70,000 × 24 months, applied for directly to NSERC as an external applicant, start April 2027 – January 2028, and every other Canadian item is either a fallback for it (DSI, REPFP, Vector), an alternative to it that cannot be held with it (CIRTA), or a top-up designed to sit beside it (Mitacs Accelerate, Branco Weiss [concurrency to confirm]).
